# Content craft for Zodiac sticker shorts

## Choose story shape and delivery separately

Do not assume every Zodiac video is a connected mini-story, and do not use “narrative mode” to mean story structure.

Two independent decisions are made before writing:

- **story shape**: how the material is organized;
- **delivery mode**: how the narrator speaks to the viewer.

Delivery modes live in `narrative-modes.md`. A Character Deconstruction can be Direct or Conversational. A Micro Story can also be Direct or Conversational. This separation prevents the new banter direction from replacing all existing formats.

## Story shapes

### Character deconstruction / contradiction ladder

Best for bóc-trần, roast, outside-vs-inside, and narrator-led personality dissection. Start from one evidence-bounded tension, then climb through distinct facets. Beats may reset into new situations; they do not need one continuous plot.

Useful tendency:
- pointed claim/contrast;
- concrete everyday enactment;
- reframe or contradiction when supported;
- cut/reset into a new facet;
- cumulative synthesis or payoff.

This is a tendency, not a required five-part template.

### Hybrid deconstruction

Use one central thesis across a few compact sketches. Each sketch contributes a new facet. The sketches should feel cumulative, not like unrelated anecdotes and not like an accidental long short film.

### Micro story

Use when a scene-led sequence best fits the approved idea. Keep it sign-specific, visually readable, and evidence-bounded. Do not require one fixed cause→consequence→payoff pattern as an eligibility test.

## Evidence/editorial boundary

Every beat must remain classifiable as:
- `SOURCE-SUPPORTED`;
- `EDITORIAL EXAGGERATION`;
- `FICTIONAL MICRO-EXAMPLE`.

Every beat also maps to one or more evidence references in the authoring narrative plan. An editorial exaggeration can intensify tone but cannot become a new astrology claim. A fictional micro-example stages an existing interpretation; it is not evidence.

Humor never grants permission to invent motive, cause, consequence, frequency, outcome, or psychological explanation.

## Spoken writing

Write for a voice, not an article. Use natural Vietnamese syntax, conversational words, varied sentence lengths, interruptions, reactions, and pauses where useful. Avoid abstract psychology, forced slang, contentese, repetitive paraphrases, and explanation that the visual already shows.

When a subtitle/reference target is supplied, transfer mechanics only: narrator stance, contrast, cadence, escalation, resets, recognition payoff. Never reuse lines, insults, or unsupported Zodiac claims.

## Hook and progression

Draft hooks with genuinely different mechanisms after story shape and delivery mode are selected. Choose for mode fit and low claim drift.

For deconstruction, each beat must add a new facet rather than another synonym for the same trait. For micro-story, each beat must advance the approved scene-led flow.

For Conversational delivery, the sign/topic appears early and riffing never delays the first substantive beat. Read aloud before handoff and check Vietnamese naturalness, sign specificity, evidence mapping, distinct beat meanings, interaction opportunities, and payoff.

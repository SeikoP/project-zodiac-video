# Publish copy: title, cover hook, caption, hashtags

Written by the agent after reading the whole episode (narration, brief, scope, writer brief), never assembled from narration fragments. The narration and its spoken hook stay as approved. Five outputs, five different jobs; none repeats another.

1. **Title** (search line, at most 100 characters): readable Vietnamese with the sign AND this episode's specific tension or search term. A complete promise, not the first spoken line, not cut to fit.
2. **Cover hook** (6-14 words, the brief's `cover.hook` unless something stronger is earned): the concrete contradiction or social consequence of this episode, supported by a real beat. Complements the title, never duplicates it. Replacing the sign with another sign must weaken it.
3. **Caption** (the text pasted into TikTok, at most 300 characters): one natural, specific line of context or an honest, slightly polarising question. Never a summary of the narration, never "comment/share" bait, never fabricated claims.
4. **Hashtags** (5-9, distinct, genuinely relevant): the sign (`#ThiênBình` style written without spaces), the zodiac niche (`#12CungHoangDao` or `#CungHoangDao`, not both), this episode's behaviour or tension, the format when it truly is one, an audience-discovery term, and `#bungmoto`. Letters, digits and underscore only after `#`. No `#fyp`, `#viral`, `#xuhuong`, no unrelated trends, no duplicates. Coverage of meaning, not a quota.
5. **Alternative hooks** (up to 3): different angles (sharp comedic provocation, character contradiction, emotional curiosity), each complete and honest, for the owner to swap in.

Choose by writing three candidates privately and keeping the one with the most concrete sign-specific promise and an accurate payoff. Edgy or satirical is allowed when earned; a bland option is not safer. No stale formulas ("bạn có biết..."). No measured lift is claimed.

Check before handing in: one sign and one tension run through title, hook, caption and tags; every claim is supported by a narration beat; nothing is empty, copied from the narration, or redundant; compare with `recent_episodes` and avoid their phrasing.

## The improvement: coherent emotional movement, not segmented storytelling
Select a single human-scale tension. Create an *emotional throughline* that changes what one observable detail means from opening to payoff. Emotional movement can be amusement → recognition → tenderness, irritation → understanding, surprise → appreciation, etc. Avoid automatically selecting melancholy, breakups, classroom or nostalgia. Use an authentic sign-related facet and a concrete context chosen by meaning, not asset convenience.

Possible levers; use selectively, never force counts or sequence:
- **Opening specificity:** a small vivid action that the viewer recognizes before any long explanation. The opening must surface sign/topic promptly.
- **Two-sided humanity:** a strength can create friction and a weakness can protect something; explore evidence-supported tension rather than a universal motive or diagnosis.
- **Choice as characterization:** a visible act of replying/not replying, sharing/holding back, waiting/leaving can tell more than a narrator assigning hidden feelings.
- **Relational perspective:** contrast how an action appears to another person and how it can be understood differently; don't invent factual consequences.
- **Quiet subtext:** let one understated beat breathe instead of directly declaring sadness or wisdom. Do not add silence that cuts off spoken audio.
- **Return with a difference:** reuse one motif (object, phrase, gesture) at the end whose meaning has genuinely changed. End on the detail when stronger than a slogan; avoid restating moral in a second line.

## Friendly Gossip B rhythm
Sound like a close friend describing a recognizable type of situation: social observation → small amused aside → concrete nuance; no required order, no banter quota. Let reactions vary. Allow lower-volume, dry, amused, sharp or warm beats when meaningful; do not require any one baseline or a return to sharpness. Do not insert obligatory confrontational argument (Delivery C), empty rhetorical questions or repeated 'ủa/khoan/nói thật'. The banter should contribute a new nuance or increase closeness to the subject. If removable with no loss, cut it.

## Scene/visual handoff
A short action or a tiny fictional scene is a manifestation inside Character, NOT independent `micro_story`. Before drawing, identify a semantic prop that actually carries meaning, and whether its visual state changes. Motif evolution example: phone held while checking messages → phone set down after a deliberate decision. No redundant per-word SVGs or floating effects without targets. ONLY spans marked as topic banter omit environment structures: token-color canvas + character + meaningful props/effects. The rest preserves existing native V4 environments and spatial contracts. Respect scene splitting if the runner cannot transition scenery safely within one scene.

## Post-write editorial review (inside existing Phase 01)
Check: (1) one tension rather than a disconnected trait list; (2) at least one concrete, specific choice; (3) distinct progression instead of paraphrase; (4) emotional turn shown through behavior, not an invented inner diagnosis; (5) motif/callback is earned, not mandatory; (6) ending leaves resonance without preaching; (7) Friendly Gossip B remains natural and confined visual rule holds; (8) factual boundaries and shape rule respected. Mark REVIEW for meaningful failures; preserve all mandatory validator receipts. No regex score can measure poignancy. Compare alternate endings when weak and choose the more cutting, earned one without forced sentiment.

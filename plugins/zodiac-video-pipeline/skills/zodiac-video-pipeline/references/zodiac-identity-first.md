# Zodiac Identity First — Content direction contract v2.4.15

## Editorial invariant
The product is **character deconstruction of a particular Zodiac archetype**. It is NOT a generic short story about life or a friendship lesson with a zodiac sign added to the title. Zodiac is an interpretive cultural/archetypal lens, not evidence of immutable real-world behavior. Avoid deterministic claims.

## Research → sign lens → premise
Before proposing situations, the Concept planner must select one **source-bounded distinctive identity mechanism**: perceived trait, less-visible counter-trait, behavioral manifestation and what this tension reveals about the chosen sign. `sign_id` and at least two resolvable source references travel with each candidate. Reject rebranded generic friendship, school, office, parenting or morality tales as the primary narrative.

Three premises MUST be different **ways to reveal the sign mechanism**, not three differently staged life stories or hooks. For each candidate, explicitly fill a *sign-replacement counterfactual*: pick a realistic other sign, explain precisely how changing the sign would change the essential reasoning and payoff, and specify why the premise cannot survive substitution unchanged. If the answer is merely changing adjectives/names/visual stickers, return to evidence and Concept.

## Deconstruction beats, not plot beats
Each major beat must introduce a new facet of the sign's selected identity tension: observable tendency → mistaken reading → counterexample or paradox → sharper reinterpretation, in a flexible order. Real-life situations, characters, dialogues and environments are **evidence-facing illustrations** only. A setting may occupy a brief beat, not dominate the thesis. Do not require a named supporting cast, episodic time jumps, a protagonist apologizing, repairing a friendship, healing trauma or learning a lesson. Do not force a particular structure or timing: a genuinely sign-specific miniature example may deserve more room when it adds a distinct facet.

## Payoff
The ending must reframe an earlier sign-specific observation. It need not improve the character morally. Comedy, dry recognition, contradiction or uncomfortable insight are valid. Forbid generic lessons that still work if the zodiac label is removed, including default 'listen to your friends', 'everyone has feelings', 'be yourself', and 'learn to grow' endings, unless unusually sign-specific reasoning is demonstrated.

## Spoken narration guardrail
In the first meaningful spoken segment (not just the title), make recognizable what is unique about this sign. Throughout the script, mark at least THREE distinct sign-based insights or behavior/interpretation shifts grounded in evidence, not three synonyms. An imaginary scene should not run more than ~one quarter of the narrative unless every section actively deconstructs the sign and the editorial reviewer records a strong exception. Do not literally say trait lists or disclaimers in dialogue. Source uncertainty stays in research/QC rather than spoken hedging.

## Review: sign-swap, moral-arc, scene dominance
Independent editor must inspect actual narration WITHOUT sign names and ask: could this be a generally applicable life lesson? Then replace the sign with a plausible different sign. If conflict, actions and payoff all remain coherent, mark REVIEW. Inspect whether more than half of the draft follows an invented external plot and only the ending names the zodiac, and whether 3 nonredundant archetype facets can be quoted. A content-quality PASS with sign-specificity unresolved is forbidden.

## Repair routing and compatibility
Keep the three phases; add no external agent, new Story Shape or change to Job@5. For new 2.4.15+ fresh runs, this contract precedes Story Quality and Narration; older approved packages remain untouched. For a requested redo, keep prior revision for comparison and restart at evidence/concept, not merely cadence editing. Never claim deterministic tests prove creative appeal; editorial judgment is explicit.

## Multi-member identity
Narrative2.1 uses episode_scope/member_lenses, not a primary sign. Source corroboration and sign-swap challenge apply to every selected member. Group traits cannot replace individual motives; three researched facets are options rather than a spoken quota. See episode-formats.md.

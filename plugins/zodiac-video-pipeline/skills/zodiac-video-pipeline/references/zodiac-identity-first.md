# Zodiac Identity First — creative contract

The organizing engine is character_deconstruction of a source-bounded Zodiac archetype. Archetypes are a cultural interpretive lens, not proof of immutable behavior. Preserve selected scope and independent corroboration; fictional examples never become evidence.

## Premise and progression
Explore genuinely different premises when the interpretation is uncertain. No fixed candidate count. A user-selected premise can stand alone with selection_mode: user_selected and a reason; record exactly the actual alternatives reserved or rejected. Each candidate preserves identity_mechanism, misread_vs_reframe, nonempty archetype_facets, source references and sign_swap_test. These legacy field names are planning context, not a compulsory misunderstanding → twist → callback formula.

Choose enough distinct facets to support the premise, without a spoken quota. A beat can advance understanding, embody a supported observation, pause, bridge or land a joke. Describe its semantic_delta honestly; do not invent another trait for a delivery beat. Review progression across the whole piece. Illustrative scenes may take the space they need when they serve the lens; no percentage limit or forced school/relationship plot. Do not append an explanation after every example.

## Opening and ending
A scene, concrete action or deadpan observation may open before naming the sign. Spoken identity must appear in the piece; absence in the first forty words is an editorial diagnostic, not automatic rejection. The ending may recognize, contrast, leave discomfort or deliver humor; no mandatory moral transformation or twist. Story Spine starting_read describes entry into the subject, and payoff_target describes the intended landing.

## Editorial review
Perform one native editorial pass against the actual draft. Check sign-swap, scene dominance and moral-arc drift, then the six story criteria. Sign-swap asks whether the selected lens and reasoning are warranted; it does not demand a human behavior exclusive to one sign. Shared behavior is possible, unsupported exclusive claims are not. A PASS can record no objection; REVIEW must identify the actual unresolved problem with grounded excerpts. Do not manufacture criticisms or excerpt diversity to fill quotas. Structural validation is not creative approval.

For Narrative2.1 use episode_scope/member_lenses without inventing a primary sign. Each selected member retains source-bounded interpretation and ranking honesty. Preserve approved narration bytes and genuine review/source fingerprints. Wording repairs do not automatically reopen research when sources and interpretation remain unchanged.

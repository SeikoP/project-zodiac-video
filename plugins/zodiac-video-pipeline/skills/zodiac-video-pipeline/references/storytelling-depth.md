# Zodiac character-led emotional storytelling — research-to-craft v2.2.32

## Authority and scope
Zodiac prohibits `micro_story` as an independent Story Shape. New work defaults to `character_deconstruction`, without alternate Story Shapes. `micro_scene`, `interaction`, concrete event, and dialog may occur inside permitted shapes as illustrative devices; a scene never becomes the governing story shape. Story Spine, evidence boundaries, existing approvals, three phases, Job@5 and runtime ownership stay unchanged. Do not convert sign symbolism into scientifically proven human behavior.

## Research synthesis (mechanics, not copy/paste scripts)
- Humanities LibreTexts, *Character and the World of Story* (2026): a character's external WANT drives action, inner NEED can drive meaningful reframing, and pressure forces revealing choices. Apply only to an invented illustration when actually needed; never assert a hidden real-life Zodiac need as evidence. https://human.libretexts.org/Courses/Laney_College/Scriptwriting_for_Video_Broadcast_and_Digital_Media_(Zeigler)/06%3A_Character_and_the_World_of_Story/6.01%3A_Character_and_the_World_of_Story
- Georgetown, *What Every Movie Must Have* (2024): an interruption of ordinary expectations generates a narrative question. For short Zodiac character content, choose one small but consequential changed expectation, not a compulsory catastrophic plot twist. https://www.georgetown.edu/news/ask-a-professor-what-every-movie-needs/
- National Centre for Writing, Jenn Ashworth interview (2022): short fiction gains strength from concentrated specificity, omission, subtext and trusting the reader rather than overexplaining the end. Prefer precise memorable behavior and an understated finish. https://nationalcentreforwriting.org.uk/writing-hub/how-to-write-a-short-story-2/
- Agrawal et al., *Narrative transportation: a systematic literature review*, Psychology & Marketing: synthesizes 95 peer-reviewed studies and distinguishes immersion, identification and affective/cognitive outcomes. This does NOT prove that any one short-video formula always increases retention. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mar.22011
- Claudia Hunter Johnson, *Crafting Short Screenplays That Connect*, Routledge: relationship, human connection and change are useful lenses for emotionally resonant short narrative. https://www.routledge.com/Crafting-Short-Screenplays-That-Connect/HunterJohnson/p/book/9781032880297

## Design guidance — character first, not a mandatory 6-step script
1. **Recognizable detail**: establish one small concrete action, choice, or social misreading supported by evidence or clearly framed fiction. Do not start with abstract sign-label adjectives alone.
2. **Hidden question**: ask what the *viewer* has misunderstood, not what secret motive the sign universally has. Support the reframe with the story's observable behavior.
3. **Gentle pressure**: let a plausible disagreement, opportunity cost, misunderstanding, or constraint sharpen the identity tension. Avoid exaggerated tragedy/trauma to manufacture feeling.
4. **Meaningful choice or boundary**: show the character choosing, stopping, delaying, or yielding; a small withheld action can reveal more than an explanatory speech. Do not insist on transformation in every concept.
5. **Relational resonance**: when justified, show what the other person can observe, or what remains unsaid; differentiate invented theatrical reaction from factual generalization.
6. **Quiet aftertaste**: make the payoff reinterpret an earlier detail or object while leaving space for the viewer. Avoid repeating the moral, generic Zodiac praise, inspirational quotes or forced tears.

Use 2–4 of these levers if they fit the concept. There is NO fixed beat number, arc, duration, reaction quota, or mandatory conflict/twist. Emotional depth is a property of choices and consequences within an evidence-safe character argument, not a separate story shape.

## Friendly Gossip B integration
- Narrator feels like a friend sharing an observation, not a courtroom debater (C) or omniscient psychologist. Natural short asides, gentle teasing, recognitions, empathetic pauses and callbacks may surround serious moments; quiet lines should not be immediately broken by jokes.
- Control contrast between warmth and silence; reserve emotionally significant statements for unhurried delivery. Writer supplies natural punctuation and rhythm, runner owns performed TTS timing and mix.
- Talk about the subject, not invented real people; staged fictional dialogue is a compact manifestation, not an independent micro-story.

## Visual mapping
Choose an anchored prop or effect only when it materially clarifies the action/subtext (unsent message, blank chair, unopened gift, question mark disappearing). Props are story participants with entered/released semantic events; avoid metaphor asset spam. For **topic-banter spans only**, no environmental/structural background, retain design-token canvas and character/prop/effect. All other facets retain their scene environment. Visual engine/runner contracts remain authoritative.

## Editorial QC before Content Review
- Was the core sign/identity tension recognized early? Did at least one behavioral detail change the audience's interpretation?
- Could the story's emotional beat still register without a narrator explaining it? If no, edit for specificity and subtext.
- Does each shift add consequence, tradeoff, relation, boundary or interpretation, rather than a synonym?
- Is the ending earned by a preceding seed/callback, with no generic lesson?
- Are emotion, inner motives, diagnosis, relationships and consequences evidence-safe or clearly fictional?
- Is shape exactly `character_deconstruction`? Any other Story Shape -> REJECT/REVISE.
- Is the banter still Delivery B, with backgroundless visuals confined only to banter spans?

Do not claim research predicts particular retention; evaluate with real viewers and production evidence later. Post-write reviewer owns these subjective checks; do not pretend regex can measure poignancy.

## Identity guardrail (v2.4.15)
The WANT/NEED, emotional change, relational resonance and quiet-aftertaste levers are **optional**, not automatic drivers for Zodiac content. They must not lead to a universal apology/relationship lesson plot. The accepted organizing principle is `zodiac-identity-first.md`: archetype facets and explanatory reinterpretation; mini-scenes must serve those facets.

# Zodiac Narrative Delivery

All NEW Zodiac uses `character_deconstruction` only. Scene, conversation or interaction are internal illustration devices, never alternative Story Shapes.

Delivery B Friendly Gossip is the default: the narrator observes, jokes, teases, reacts, argues playfully and deepens sign-specific identity tension. Sharp comedic intensity is permitted. Avoid generic filler, forced classroom settings, mandatory banter quotas and uniform melancholy endings.

A beat may add observation, recognizable action, contradiction, social friction, insight or payoff, but no fixed order is enforced. Every beat requires a semantic delta and evidence mapping. Contradictions require support and rationale. Content QC reviews voice/personality, depth, specificity, payoff and density. Schema validation proves structure, not that writing is compelling. See `character-viral-performance.md` and `topic-banter-layer.md`.


## Narrative center (v2.4.15)
Use `zodiac-identity-first.md` as the authority when scenes dominate. The deconstruction must remain true even if all incidental classmates, friends and settings are removed; do not mandate an external plot or corrective moral arc.

## Narrative archetype overlay

`character_deconstruction` remains the only Story Shape. A **narrative archetype** is not a new Story Shape and does not replace Zodiac identity; it is an optional world/delivery grammar that makes the selected behavior legible through form.

Examples include detective case, QA/bug tracker, interrogation, mock documentary, POV reconstruction, countdown, trial, sports commentary or a custom mode. Do not create a sign→mode lookup table. Rank the mode from the episode's actual mechanics.

Prefer **implicit viewer inference**: show the clue-reading, bug-checking, cross-examining or reconstructing behavior strongly enough that viewers can name the role themselves. Do not automatically put the role label in narration, cover or caption.

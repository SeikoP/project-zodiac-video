# Content Quality Engine — premise before performance (v2.4.13)

New, fresh Phase-01 authoring MUST establish **why this particular story is worth watching before polishing Vietnamese delivery**. Maintain exactly three existing phases and no new sub-agent, shape or runtime payload. `character_deconstruction` is the only Zodiac Story Shape; scenes, dialogue and decisions are devices. An observational sketch can win without a large plot twist if its specific recognition, escalating insight and reframe are genuinely distinctive. Do not force a dramatic formula, school scene or moral lesson on every sign.

## Concept competition (Concept step, before Viral hook optimization)
1. Using verified evidence and the selected sign, generate THREE distinct candidate *premises* (not three rewrites of the same hook) in `.authoring/concept-candidates.json`. Each candidate identifies `character_specificity`, `unanswered_question`, `stakes_or_recognition`, `turn_or_reframe`, `why_watch_without_delivery`, `failure_risk`, and `evidence_refs`. Preserve relevant source boundaries. Name one winner, explicitly reject or reserve the others. Avoid reusing completed-series concepts.
2. Run the candidate/lineage section of `validate-content-contract.mjs`; for a 2.4.13 fresh run the absence of the artifact or a selected-concept match is a hard failure. The validator checks STRUCTURE AND PROVENANCE; it cannot decide whether a joke or premise is compelling.
3. Make `story_spine` describe the winner's curiosity/question, the cost or recognition at risk, the meaningful change and the earned final reframe. `narrative-plan.json.selected_concept_id` must point to the winner. A premise may be observation-led; its stakes can be social friction or recognition rather than theatrical disaster.
4. Only now choose optional Viral support. Use it for hook/intensity optimization **after** the story point, never to rescue an uninteresting story with false mystery. Video Lab techniques are optional guidance and must not force per-second asset churn, false retention claims or external affiliate content into Narration.

## Independent editorial challenge (Narration step, after the full draft)
Create `.authoring/story-quality-review.json` separate from `narrative-qc.json`. In a clean editorial-review pass, inspect actual Narration against the chosen premise. For each of these criteria, identify **one concrete weakness that an unfriendly viewer could reasonably raise**, then defend or revise with grounded spoken excerpts: `premise_strength`, `stakes_or_recognition`, `progression_and_turn`, `sign_irreplaceability`, `payoff_and_callback`, `silent_read_test`. `silent_read_test` means imagine plain text with no TTS, music, motion or captions: is there still a clear reason to read/watch through the ending? A reviewer MUST mark `REVIEW` when a material objection remains; no automatic PASS and no fabricated independent review agent.

The deterministic `validate-story-quality-review.mjs` verifies source fingerprints, selected concept, distinct grounded excerpts, objection-specific notes and decisions; **its PASS only certifies a well-formed review artifact**. Human/LLM editorial judgment is still necessary and Content Review remains a real user approval. Avoid numerical virality/retention guarantees and fake benchmarks.

## Repair routing
If defects concern premises, stakes, cause/effect, same-sign substitution, weak reveal or a predictable ending, return to Concept, revise candidates and Story Spine, then rewrite Narration. If premise is sound and only speech cadence/wording is weak, revise Narration without altering the approved concept. Preserve any already approved scripts; a requested revision reopens Content Review only for that script. Fresh restart never silently overwrites approved content.

## Compatibility
Artifacts are mandatory for fresh runs whose pipeline-state `plugin_version` is 2.4.13 or later. Older reviewed jobs and their receipts are not retroactively invalidated; migration/restart to this version must revalidate materially modified content. Never mark old Narrative QC as proof of story quality.


## Direction Priority v2.4.15 — Zodiac-first, not plot-first
**Read `references/zodiac-identity-first.md` before creating candidate premises.** Story strength and viewer curiosity are secondary to and dependent on archetype specificity. Reject generic plot-led concepts even when stakes/twists are good. Three candidates explore three distinct ways of deconstructing the chosen sign; each must document `identity_mechanism`, `misread_vs_reframe`, `archetype_facets` (three nonredundant facets), `sign_swap_test` (plausible alternate sign + irreplaceable difference) and `scene_dependence_risk`. A 'redemption' or 'friendship moral' ending is never compulsory. Review `sign_irreplaceability`, `scene_dominance`, and `moral_arc_drift` before the prior six editorial criteria; unresolved issues reopen Concept and block Content Review. New 2.4.15+ reviews use `zodiac_identity_review` with the three criteria and evidence-grounded excerpts; the validator checks artifact structure and marks these reviews mandatory, not creative merit.

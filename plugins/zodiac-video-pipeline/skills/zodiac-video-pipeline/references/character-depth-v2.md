# Character-first Story Shape — single policy

New Zodiac authoring permits exactly ONE Story Shape: `character_deconstruction`. Both `micro_story` and `hybrid_deconstruction` are forbidden as shapes. An imagined scene, dialogue or event can only be a device within Character. Old user-approved work is not silently migrated or relabeled.

## Story construction
Select a recognizable sign-specific identity tension bounded by evidence. Declare the Story Spine (starting read, progression, payoff) before Viral Analysis. Every beat must add a meaningful semantic delta. Use concrete behavior, contradiction escalation, narrator reactions, tension, reframing and an earned ending. No mandatory beat count, movie-plot sequence or forced sadness. Delivery B Friendly Gossip may be provocative, humorous, teasing and emotional, but never substitutes fictional claims for evidence.

See `character-viral-performance.md` for depth, emotional velocity, callback and current 90–120-second editorial target. Keep approved Narration locked. Only topic-banter spans omit environment; remaining native V4 environments retain semantic scene positions.


## Non-generic Zodiac focus
Read `zodiac-identity-first.md` before 'character development' techniques. Zodiac archetype deconstruction, not a generic fictional arc, is the primary product. Require distinct facets and an earned sign-specific reframe.

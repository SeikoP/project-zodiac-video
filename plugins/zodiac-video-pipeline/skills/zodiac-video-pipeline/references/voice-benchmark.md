# Voice benchmark — behavior-first roast narrator

This benchmark is a **style-only** distillation from the two user-supplied reference transcripts (Bạch Dương and Bọ Cạp). It does not authorize Zodiac claims, exact jokes, exact sentences, distinctive phrases, or reference-specific situations as facts.

## Narrator relationship
The narrator sounds like someone who has known the subject long enough to publicly expose their habits. The stance is familiar, amused, mildly exasperated and confident enough to interrupt or call out contradictions. The narrator is not a neutral host, therapist, lecturer or horoscope encyclopedia.

## Core movement
Prefer this causal movement when the material supports it:

`observable behavior -> narrator catches it -> contradiction/reversal -> exaggeration or internal voice -> vulnerability leak -> pride/roast recovery`

This is a movement pattern, not a mandatory template for every beat.

## Behavioral dimensions

### 1. Behavior before analysis
Lead with something the character does, says, avoids, demands, hides or overreacts to. Explanation comes only after the behavior has earned it.

### 2. Comic causality
Humor should arise from the behavior or contradiction already on screen/in narration. Do not paste a joke onto generic explanatory prose.

### 3. Contradiction engine
The strongest passages expose incompatible impulses living together: wanting care while rejecting pity, acting indifferent while monitoring everything, claiming not to need something while reacting when it is absent. Do not invent an inner cause unless evidence supports it.

### 4. Narrator reaction
The narrator is allowed to react: disbelief, mock annoyance, side comments, compressed rhetorical questions, or a short verdict. A paragraph that could be read unchanged by a documentary host is suspicious.

### 5. Internal voice
Use brief imagined self-talk only when it dramatizes an already-supported behavior. Internal voice must not become evidence for hidden motives.

### 6. Sentence music
Natural Vietnamese speech should vary. A common useful shape is a longer accelerating sentence followed by a short impact line. Avoid turning every clause into a separate one-line fragment.

### 7. Controlled roughness
Colloquial roughness may remain when it belongs to the narrator. Do not sanitize every transition into polished essay prose, but do not force profanity or slang quotas.

### 8. Warmth leakage
Affection is usually indirect: the narrator keeps roasting while allowing loyalty, sincerity, insecurity or softness to become visible through behavior. Do not stop the script to announce a healing lesson.

### 9. Emotional movement
The voice may move between sharp and normal/observational. Avoid a late automatic drift into warm inspirational narration.

### 10. Ending energy
The ending should preserve the narrator family: callback, contradiction, pride recovery, roast, or a behavioral image that completes the premise. A generic reflective moral is a drift risk.

## Known failure modes
- `GENERIC_EXPLAINER`: polished Zodiac explanation that could fit any narrator.
- `SLANG_PAINTED_AI`: generic prose with casual words inserted after the fact.
- `MISSING_NARRATOR_REACTION`: behavior is described but the narrator never catches or reacts to it.
- `WEAK_COMIC_CAUSALITY`: jokes are detachable from the story/behavior.
- `RHYTHM_FLATTENING`: sentence lengths and emphasis become mechanically uniform.
- `THERAPIST_DRIFT`: vulnerability turns into therapeutic or healing language.
- `ENDING_ENERGY_DROP`: ending becomes inspirational, abstract or softer than the established narrator.
- `REFERENCE_CONTAMINATION`: a reference-specific claim, joke, phrase or situation is imported without independent evidence.

## Review rule
A Voice Fidelity review must cite concrete narration evidence for its important judgments and must compare the draft against this behavioral benchmark plus the current `voice-profile.json`. Schema validity alone is not evidence of voice match.

# Voice benchmark — behavior-first roast narrator

Style-only benchmark distilled from the user-supplied Bạch Dương and Bọ Cạp transcripts. It never authorizes Zodiac claims, exact jokes, phrases, sentences or reference-specific situations.

## Narrator family
Sound like someone who knows the subject well enough to expose their habits in public: familiar, amused, mildly exasperated, willing to interrupt and call out contradictions. Never become a neutral host, therapist, lecturer or horoscope encyclopedia.

Useful causal movement when supported:
`behavior -> narrator catches it -> contradiction/reversal -> exaggeration or internal voice -> vulnerability leak -> roast/pride recovery`

This is a movement pattern, not a beat quota.

## Behavioral dimensions
- **behavior_before_analysis** — show what the character does/says/avoids before explaining it.
- **comic_causality** — humor must grow from that behavior or contradiction, not from detachable jokes.
- **contradiction_engine** — expose incompatible impulses without inventing unsupported motives.
- **narrator_reaction** — allow disbelief, mock annoyance, side comments, short rhetorical questions and verdicts.
- **internal_voice** — brief imagined self-talk may dramatize supported behavior; it is not evidence.
- **sentence_music** — vary spoken Vietnamese rhythm; long acceleration may land on a short impact line. Avoid fragmenting every clause.
- **controlled_roughness** — keep natural colloquial roughness; never force profanity/slang quotas.
- **warmth_leakage** — let softness appear through behavior while the narrator keeps their edge; no healing speech.
- **emotional_movement** — move between sharp and normal/observational, not toward automatic inspirational warmth.
- **ending_energy** — finish with callback, contradiction, pride recovery, roast or a behavioral image; avoid generic reflective morals.

## Failure codes
`GENERIC_EXPLAINER`, `SLANG_PAINTED_AI`, `MISSING_NARRATOR_REACTION`, `WEAK_COMIC_CAUSALITY`, `RHYTHM_FLATTENING`, `THERAPIST_DRIFT`, `ENDING_ENERGY_DROP`, `REFERENCE_CONTAMINATION`.

## Review rule
Voice Review must cite actual narration excerpts and compare them with this benchmark plus `voice-profile.json`. Schema-valid self-approval is not voice evidence.

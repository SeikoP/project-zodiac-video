# Topic banter / conversational delivery

Choose the narrator stance from the premise and explicit user direction. Friendly gossip, dry observation, direct explanation, playful roast and dramatic contrast are available textures; no universal gossip baseline or emotion quota. Preserve delivery_mode: direct | conversational and character_deconstruction.

Optional moves include a specific observation, aside, viewer question, scenelet, contrast and callback. Use them where they add voice or recognition, not in a recurring mandatory sequence. An imagined scene is an illustration, never evidence of motives or outcomes. Remove interchangeable chatter, repeated reaction filler and tangents that obscure the selected lens. No obligatory rebuttal, misunderstanding or reassuring ending.

Keep compact narrator/rhythm direction in the writer brief. Review the actual spoken draft for evidence boundaries, coherent progression and payoff. Structural PASS cannot certify appeal.

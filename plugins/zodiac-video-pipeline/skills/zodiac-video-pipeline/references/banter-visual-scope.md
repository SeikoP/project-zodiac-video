# Banter-only visual scope (v2.2.31) — authoritative

## Boundary
This rule applies ONLY to identifiable `topic_banter` moments or spans within character-led narration. Other content layers, including identity analysis, behavior illustration, contradiction, relational examples and payoff, retain the currently chosen V4 environment/background and scene continuity; DO NOT disable backgrounds globally. Neutral design-token canvas/base color remains for banter; no room, classroom, street, structural environmental cue or scenery SVG in those spans. Do not treat absence of scenery as a reason to omit character/prop/effect.

## Semantics-first composition
- Delivery B / Friendly Gossip: narrator warmly chats with the viewer about the current Zodiac trait; conversational recognition, light teasing and social detail without enforced aggressive rebuttal. Story Shape remains `character_deconstruction`.
- On banter moments use character action/expression plus prop/effect: e.g. character glances at phone, a question-mark effect appears beside the character at the voiced question, then clears; doodle/thought effect or reaction lines may punctuate a supported observation.
- Props and effects may enter/exit or react with explicit semantic events, target, supported motion preset and duration. The effect lifecycle starts hidden, enters on an event, and releases on another; spatial/contact anchors and user-approved actor identity still apply.
- Do not invent a full alternate set or extra SVG per spoken phrase. Use existing catalog assets first. Preserve readable captions and the design token's open space.
- If banter and non-banter share a scene, isolate the visual treatment to the authored banter beat/span; return to the original environment for non-banter without global scene clearing or accidental location change. Where runtime cannot support a mid-scene environment-state change safely, separate into logical adjacent scenes retaining source narration and actor continuity; test event and transition coherence. Do not claim a native preview or runtime parity PASS without actual evidence.

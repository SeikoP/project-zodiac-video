# Visual Metaphor Contract

Visual Metaphor is a scene-contained visual expression of narration meaning. It is not a background and is not automatically a physical prop.

Fresh plans use `zodiac-visual-metaphor-plan@1`. A metaphor may define one or more named `visual_states`; cues bind those states to real narration anchors. Stateful metaphors may transition, for example Libra `tilt_left → balanced → tilt_right`, but no universal setup/friction/payoff lifecycle is required.

Physical contact remains governed by Prop/Contact contracts. Effects remain optional support.

# Character Viral Performance — v2.3.0
This is the single editorial decision layer for all new Zodiac videos. Zodiac has ONE Story Shape: `character_deconstruction`. Mini-scenes, reactions, dialogue and interactions are illustration devices only; never promote them to separate shapes. Delivery B Friendly Gossip retains the Story Shape, but its channel-wide default is **TONE_RHYTHM_DEFAULT**: direction follows semantic beats, with no fixed intensity.

## Channel voice — TONE_RHYTHM_DEFAULT (authoritative)
The narrator remains everyday, perceptive, witty and sharp when the beat earns it. Choose delivery, energy, emotion, pace and emphasis by this beat's semantic change; do not impose high energy, an aggressive baseline, mandatory softening or a required emotional sequence. Lower-volume, understated or warm beats may stand on their own. No obligatory return to a sharper beat. **Không tự động làm dịu** a chosen cutting punchline; equally, never turn a quiet observation into a roast just to satisfy a channel default.

`TONE_GAT_DEFAULT` is a legacy choice for existing approved work, not the fresh default. Preserve evidence boundaries and bytes of user-approved Narration. Apply new direction only to fresh content or an explicitly authorized revision. Read `narration-rhythm.md` for Narrative Plan v2 and actual runner capability limits.

## Bring the character alive
Build one evidence-bounded identity tension, recognizable behavior and a point-of-view narrator. Develop a contradiction in meaningful turns: an outside read -> revealing action -> social friction or cost -> deeper counter-reading. Each turn must change the audience's interpretation; it must not simply repeat a stronger adjective. No required number of turns or section template.
Narrator may banter, tease, exaggerate theatrically, switch pace, argue briefly, use colloquial Vietnamese and deliver a sharp character roast. DO NOT auto-soften strong expressions because of intensity. Make sure an audience can distinguish performance exaggeration from factual diagnosis, universal sign claims, or attacks on real people.
A single concrete relatable action is stronger than a list of adjectives. Character specificity must remain tied to this sign's selected tension and documented evidence.

## Emotional velocity and endings
Choose the emotional trajectory for THIS topic: comedic -> fierce -> vulnerable -> warm; irritation -> insight; playful -> defiant; or quiet -> poignant. These are examples, NOT required sequences. Avoid default melancholy and compulsory tear-jerker endings.
Return to an earlier phrase, gesture or prop only if it gains meaning. Strong endings may be triumphant, spicy, funny, disputable, tender, or understated. Don't tack on a moral or redundant explanation after the earned payoff.
Every substantive banter line should advance recognition, contrast, connection or reaction; no generic warm-up lines or narrator tic quota.

## Hook & viral mechanics
Story Spine + evidence FIRST. Viral analysis SECOND to create stronger entry, contrast, punch lines, curiosity, callback and comment-provoking payoff, NEVER to change Story Shape or invent factual Zodiac psychology.
Review an intentionally strong hook alongside the restrained hook. Keep the stronger one when it is clear, anchored and actually supports the narrative promise; do not reflexively pick the milder one.
Provocation, edgy colloquial phrasing and memorable rhetorical exaggeration are valid creative tools, but made-up statistics, alleged personal experiences and unsupported universal claims are not evidence.

## Duration and speech density
Editorial target: **90–120 seconds**, preferred **100–110 seconds including pauses and ending tail**. This replaces the old 2m15s–2m45s guidance. Never pad for duration; don't accelerate speech to disguise an overlong script. A 90–120s estimate is a Phase-01 editorial requirement; a deviation requires a specifically recorded user exception and new Content Review. Estimate whitespace-separated Vietnamese tokens at an explicit baseline speaking rate (default 285 tokens/minute), plus expressive pause allowance and end-tail. Run `references/short-form-duration.mjs` assessment, retain the numerical estimate in the content-review artifact, and revise an estimate over 120s before approval.
Measured TTS duration, real pauses, caption timing, cover/end-tail render and the final 90–120s media QA belong to local runner. Old approved narration is immutable; user-requested shortening is a **new revision** with fresh Content Review and subsequent Visual Review.
Use dynamic cadence: accelerating banter, clearer lines at emotional reversal, space after payoff, no unmotivated silence.

## Phase 01 editorial QC, no fake score
- Hook makes the audience curious about this identity tension.
- Strong narrator language carries recognizable character insight, not interchangeable insult.
- Contradiction deepens through meaningful behavior and cause-free interpretation, without invented motives.
- At least one angle helps viewers recognize themselves or someone they know.
- Emotion changes naturally, not forced into sadness.
- Payoff resolves or provocatively reinterprets opening and is not followed by an unnecessary explanation.
- Spoken duration estimate is recorded and judged for information density, not minute quota.
- Sign-specific evidence boundaries remain. `micro_story` and `hybrid_deconstruction` as Story Shape are REJECTED.
- User-approved Story Spine/narration remain locked. Existing receipts and Content Review remain mandatory.

## Object-led visual thinking

This is a creative intent, not a license to rewrite an approved Narration or demand additional SVGs.

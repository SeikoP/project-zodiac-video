# Cast Routing v4

New ordinary characters are routed through native Character Assembly. First derive the character’s psychological tension, narrative function, contrast to the supporting cast and performed actions from the approved Narration, and record those reasons as `design_intent` in `zodiac-character-assembly-plan@2`. Only THEN decide body silhouette, hair, face profile, accessory and outfit; assign a shirt color from the native palette, keeping colors distinct among visible cast members when possible. Pose/expression are state-only; all identity traits stay fixed for the character.

## Hair is an authored identity decision

For fresh intent-bearing plans, `hair_id` must be explicit. Do not let deterministic fallback choose the hairstyle. Select hair from the character's role and silhouette needs, not from a habitual "female lead" default. The feminine pool deliberately includes bob/lob, waves, long loose hair, braids, bun, half-up and ponytail families.

Use distinct `body_profile` values for simultaneously visible recurring people when practical. Hair/outfit differences do not substitute for silhouette diversity. Likewise, co-visible recurring people should not share the same `hair_id` accidentally. If matching hair is narratively intentional, each affected character must include a meaningful `design_intent.hair_reuse_reason`.

Historical V3 character lineage is provenance only. Re-author any user-supplied specialist reference as V4 geometry; do not copy or relabel an old SVG.

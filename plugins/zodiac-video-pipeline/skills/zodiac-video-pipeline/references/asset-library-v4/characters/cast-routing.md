# Cast Routing v4

New ordinary characters are routed through native Character Assembly. First derive the character’s psychological tension, narrative function, contrast to the supporting cast and performed actions from the approved Narration, and record those reasons as `design_intent` in `zodiac-character-assembly-plan@2`. Only THEN decide body silhouette, hair, face profile, accessory and outfit; assign a shirt color from the native palette, keeping colors distinct among visible cast members when possible. Pose/expression are state-only; all identity traits stay fixed for the character.

Use distinct `body_profile` values for simultaneously visible recurring people when practical. Hair/outfit differences do not substitute for silhouette diversity.

Historical V3 character lineage is provenance only. Re-author any user-supplied specialist reference as V4 geometry; do not copy or relabel an old SVG.

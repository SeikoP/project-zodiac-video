# Choose a story engine from behavior, not a template

This is **Phase 1 editorial guidance** for Zodiac Video Web, not a new native Story Shape, JSON contract, renderer, or automatic approval stage. `character_deconstruction` remains the only producer Story Shape. This skill supplements the Zodiac Video Web entrypoint and does not replace Evidence -> Voice -> Concept -> Narrative Design -> Narration -> Content Review.

## Trigger

Apply when the user asks for a different structure, stronger "bóc phốt"/dí dỏm tone, more engaging progression, or a reasoned choice between storytelling approaches. For an already selected concept or sign, preserve its central premise and previously accepted lines unless the user changes them. Do not ask for routine confirmations or turn every episode into a format menu.

## Editorial selection, before rewriting

Describe the actual **behavioral contradiction**, why it is distinctive for the selected Zodiac lens, what changes under pressure, and what the audience should infer. Check broad recognition, sign-swap counterfactual, generic moral drift, and whether the story would still work after removing its incidental setting. Apply the Web entrypoint's Audience Alignment Gate before choosing evidence or a story engine: select viewer POV, challenge cumulative role bias, and favor self-identification for sign-led episodes unless the user or premise calls for another POV. The channel's female-majority profile is not a license to stereotype.

Consider different narrative engines only when they materially differ in how the viewer discovers the contradiction. Three useful candidates are:

- **Escalating Roast:** one accusation, increasingly consequential evidence, an earned reversal or callback. Escalation must change the stakes or interpretation, not just add three examples of the same trait.
- **Defense Collapse:** the character denies an observable contradiction; each defense is falsified or narrowed by a more revealing counterexample until their own behavior becomes the punchline. With single-voice TTS, attribute every quotation and narrate most of the exchanges. Avoid a literal courtroom scene by default.
- **Reverse Reveal:** open with a strange outcome, reconstruct how it happened using grounded actions, and let the last clue change the meaning of the opening. Never manufacture mystery where only a generic behavior exists.

Direct A/B comparison, an observed mini-sketch, or a unique format may beat all three. **Do not encode a sign-to-format lookup or insist on a three-way contest for every episode.** Choose one structure for the actual premise, using brief comparative demos only when the user requests exploration. Record the mechanism and reason in the Writer Brief/Narrative Design prose without adding mandatory fields or changing Narrative Plan 2.1.

## Roast without flat listicles

The narrator may sound like a friend uncovering receipts, teasing from observable actions rather than claiming hidden motives. For romance, contrast ordinary behavior with how a person **voluntarily** makes room for someone important; preserve their identity. Invitations are one option, not the default evidence. Use the intended viewer POV to choose varied choices and consequences rather than assuming who organizes dates, initiates, pays, or receives attention. Let contradictions, consequences, failed excuses and changed choices create jokes. Do not add unrelated punchlines, humiliating labels or proof-free psychological claims. Do not make every example an appointment or text message.

Before Narration, write a short **second-half pulse** where each later beat changes the evidence, decision, social consequence, viewer interpretation or payoff. For Defense Collapse, the last defense must fail for a new reason; merely repeating the first accusation is not an ending. Treat a recurring phrase/object as a narrative motif only if its meaning changes and it passes a removal test.

## Voice and visual controls

Write for **one Vietnamese narrator** first. Make spoken subjects, quotes, time order and causal links unambiguous; keep performance cues in the approved beat plan, not the narration text. The spoken script must survive a muted-video listen and a silent text read. Comedy should not require TTS to perform different characters or depend on editing to supply the point.

Explore original `story_visual` candidates for the episode's meaning, including nonliteral metaphor, exaggeration, symbolic imagery and visual humor. A physical situation or before/change/consequence is not required. Normally select a compelling image; omit only after the candidate review in story-visual-direction.md. No per-scene quota, duplicate captions or decorative filler.

## Review boundary

After the script, perform editorial review of hook, recognition, Audience Alignment (viewer POV and cumulative role bias), sign identity, semantic progression, comic escalation, 2nd-half pulse, callback and single-voice TTS clarity. Show the revised exact narration and the chosen mechanism. **Only a real explicit user Content Review approval may create an approval receipt or allow a Phase-1 handoff ZIP.** This skill never authorizes Visual Review, local production, or MP4 checklist completion.
